Aníkúlápò's Afrobeat

This book delves into the rich history and cultural significance of the genre-defining music of the legendary Fela Anikulapo-Kuti. It charts the origin, evolution and impact of Afrobeat on Nigerian culture and society, and its role in shaping the political and social landscape of Nigeria and Africa in the 1970s.

It also examines the power of Fela's live performances, how Afrobeat became the soundtrack of the struggle, and the lasting impact of the genre on contemporary music. The final chapter draws a clear distinction between “Afrobeat” and the modern “Afrobeats.”

A must-read for music enthusiasts, cultural historians and anyone seeking to understand the legacy of Afrobeat and its political and cultural significance.
